Open a normal website, then click ShareSafe again. Chrome blocks extensions on its settings, new-tab pages, the Web Store, and some built-in viewers.

For a local file, turn on Allow access to file URLs in ShareSafe’s extension details. Reload the file and try again.

For browser controls or other apps, use the ShareSafe desktop app and share your whole display.

On supported websites, Direct Blur is the default. Choose Style: Solid for opaque masks. Both appear on your own screen.
